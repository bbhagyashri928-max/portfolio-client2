“Education is the most powerful instrument we have to prepare compassionate, competent and courageous nurses who will shape the future of healthcare.”

It is my privilege and honour to welcome you to the Government B.Sc. Nursing College, Jalna — a premier institution established under the Public Health Department, Government of Maharashtra, and affiliated to Maharashtra University of Health Sciences (MUHS), Nashik.

Our college was established with a clear and noble mission: to provide quality nursing education to students from all sections of society, especially from rural and underserved communities. We believe that every student who walks through our doors carries the potential to transform lives — of patients, families and communities.

At Government B.Sc. Nursing College, Jalna, we are committed to building an environment of academic excellence, clinical competence and strong professional values. Our dedicated faculty, well-equipped skills laboratory, strong hospital affiliations and vibrant student activities ensure that our students receive a holistic, well-rounded education that prepares them not just for examinations, but for the realities and responsibilities of nursing practice.

We emphasise the development of critical thinking, communication, empathy and leadership — qualities that distinguish a truly skilled nurse from a merely trained one. We encourage every student to engage deeply with their learning, participate in community health activities, and uphold the highest standards of professional ethics and patient dignity.

To every student, I say with conviction:
Come with curiosity. Learn with dedication. Care with compassion. Serve with pride.

The nursing profession is one of the most noble and impactful in the world. Each one of you has chosen a path of service that will touch countless lives. I urge you to embrace this responsibility wholeheartedly, to continuously update your knowledge and skills, and to always remember that at the heart of nursing is the human being in your care.

To our faculty, staff and all stakeholders — your dedication, guidance and support are the foundation upon which our students build their futures. Together, we are building an institution of excellence, integrity and compassion.

Let us walk together on this path of knowledge, service and transformation.

With warm regards and best wishes,
Mr. Manoj L. Deshmukh

About Mr. Deshmukh

A Visionary Leader in Education
